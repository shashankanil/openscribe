OpenScribe v0.6.1 lets AI apps see your screenshots and meeting recordings, adds mouse buttons as shortcuts, and tidies the sidebar. It installs itself through the in-app updater.

### Screenshots and recordings in AI apps

- OpenScribe Cloud now keeps your screenshots and meeting recordings next to your notes and meetings, so claude.ai, ChatGPT and other connected apps can look at a screenshot or fetch a recording.
- Screenshots are sent as images up to 2048 px. Recordings are mixed into one compressed file of about 20 MB per hour.
- Choose what's included in Settings → AI → Connect AI apps. Turning a type off removes those files from the cloud, and **Delete cloud data** still removes everything.
- The on-this-Mac connection can show screenshots too, and points AI apps to recordings on disk.
- Links to recordings expire after an hour and only work for your account.

### Mouse buttons as shortcuts

- Use a mouse's side buttons (Mouse 4, Mouse 5 and more) for any action, including push to talk. Hold a side button to talk, or double-tap it to keep going hands-free.
- Middle click works too when combined with ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃.
- Click + next to an action and press the button, or choose Mouse 4 or Mouse 5 from the ⋯ menu. Mouse shortcuts need Accessibility access.

### A tidier sidebar

- Ten sidebar items are now five: **Notes**, **Meetings** (with Calendar), **Preferences** (General, Shortcuts, Personalization), **AI** (API & Models, Connect AI apps) and **Privacy & Access** (Privacy, Permissions).
- The section you're in opens to show its pages. Click it again to fold it.

### Install

OpenScribe 0.6.0 updates itself: choose **Install and relaunch** when it offers, or quit the app to install on the way out. On older versions, download the DMG or ZIP below and replace the app in Applications. Settings, keys, notes and meetings stay where they are.

Requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later. The app is signed with the same Apple Development identity as earlier releases and is not notarized.
